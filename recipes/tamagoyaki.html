Simple Rice Omelet

The simple and quick breakfast, lunch, or dinner recipes for the lazy ones

Ingredients

  • Eggs, four eggs for 6 servings
  • 1/4 cup prepared dashi stock
  • 1 tbsp white sugar
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp vegetable oil, or more as necessary

Directions

  1. Beat eggs in bowl. Whisk dashi, sugar, and soy sauce until sugar has dissolved
  2. Grease tamago pan and heat over medium heat
  3. Pour a thin layer of egg mixture. Cook until egg layer is fuirm on the bottom but liquid on top
  4. Lift up one edge using spatula and roll up the egg layer
  5. Push omelet to one side. Oil the skillet again and pour in another thin layer of egg, lifting the first omelet roll up slightly to allow th egg to flow underneath.
  6. Roll up the first omelet in the new layer of egg and push omelet to the edge of the skillet as before
  7. Repeat the process with the remaining egg mixture, oiling the pan each time if needed.
  8. Remove the rolled omelet to a serving platter and cut into 6 pieces to serve.
