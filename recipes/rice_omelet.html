Rice Omelet

Simple Rice Omelet

The simple and quick breakfast, lunch, or dinner recipes for the lazy ones

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • 2 or 3 eggs
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Wash the rice around 3 times. Cook in the rice cooker.
  2. Beat eggs in a bowl.
  3. Preheat non-stick pan over medium
  4. Pour the egg mixture
  5. Add salt and pepper to taste
  6. Stir and shake the egg mixture in the to the desired firmness
  7. Serve in a dish on top of the rice and enjoy!
