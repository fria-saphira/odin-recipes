Our Lasagna

The simple Lasagna recipe for anyone with short time and for the lazy ones

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 jar spaghetti sauce
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  • 2 tsp dried parsley
  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • ground black pepper & salt to taste
  • 1/2 cup water

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 175 degrees C
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook and stir ground beef in the skillet until browned. Stir in spaghetti sauce and simmer for 5 minutes
  3. Combine cottahe cheese, mozzarella, eggs, parsley, salt, and pepper in a large bowl
  4. Spread 3/4 cup of sauce in baking dish. Cover with 3 uncooked lasagna noodles, cheese mixture, 1/4 cup sauce; repeat layers. Pour 1/2 cup water along the edges of the dish, cover tightly with alumunium foil.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 45 minutes. Unciover and bake for an additional 10 minutes. Let stand 10 minutes before serving
  6. Serve and enjoy!
